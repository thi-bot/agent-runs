    Content metrics

    How we calculate our metrics

    Words

    We extract the main content from the page preview, remove all formatting, and calculate the number of words.

    Reading time

    We calculate reading time from word count, using different reading speeds for different languages.

    Readability

    We use a readability formula based on length of words and sentences. Long sentences and words result in a worse readability score.

    See our content checks guide for more information on how content metrics are calculated.
