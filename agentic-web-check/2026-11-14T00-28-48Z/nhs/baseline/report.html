Methodology

Every check has a status (pass = 1, warn = 0.5, fail = 0; na and info are not scored) and a weight that follows the Lighthouse accessibility scale: 10 critical, 7 serious, 3 moderate, 1 minor. A dimension score is round(100 × Σ(score × weight) / Σ(weight)) over its scorable checks; a dimension with none is n/a and excluded.

The overall score is the weighted mean of the available dimensions. Dimension weights (scaled by 0.7 because behavioural tasks were run):

  • perception: 20 × 0.7 = 14
  • navigation: 15 × 0.7 = 10.5
  • interaction: 20 × 0.7 = 14
  • machine-interfaces: 10 × 0.7 = 7
  • reliability: 15 × 0.7 = 10.5
  • safety: 20 × 0.7 = 14
  • task-success: 30

Task success = 100 × PASS / (PASS + FAIL + BLOCKED); INCONCLUSIVE runs are excluded, as is a task with safety: consequential that was BLOCKED only because the run had no --allow-consequential. A dimension score is only as broad as its scorable checks (the Scored column). Methodology version 1; see docs/SCORING.md in the project for the full catalogue.
